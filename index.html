About

I am an AI researcher working on deep learning for medical imaging, with a focus on making models reliable enough for clinical use. My work treats uncertainty as a core design element: selective prediction, evidential deep learning, conformal prediction, and federated learning that generalizes across hospitals and imaging sites.

I hold a BSc in Artificial Intelligence and Big Data from Woosong University, Daejeon (CGPA 3.85/4.5), where I work as a Research Assistant under Dr. Jia Uddin.
